## About Me

I am currently a doctoral fellow advised by [Prof. Marco Hutter](https://mavt.ethz.ch/people/person-detail.hutter.html) and [Prof. Andreas Krause](https://las.inf.ethz.ch/krausea) at [ETH AI Center](https://ai.ethz.ch/), Switzerland. From April to September 2026, I was a Student Researcher in the [Gemini Robotics](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini-robotics/) team at [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/).

### Research

My research interests focus on algorithms that can enable autonomous agents to acquire complex behaviors through learning, especially general-purpose methods that could enable any autonomous system to learn to solve any task. This spans online learning and world modeling.

### Previously

I worked on motion representation and active learning methods at [Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)](https://www.mit.edu/) with [Prof. Sangbae Kim](https://meche.mit.edu/people/faculty/SANGBAE@MIT.EDU), and on motion imitation from limited demonstrations and intrinsic skill exploration at [Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems (MPI-IS)](https://is.mpg.de/) with [Prof. Georg Martius](https://al.is.mpg.de/person/gmartius).
